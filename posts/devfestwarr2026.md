## Introduction
On Saturday October 17, 2026, the <a href="https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-warri/" target="_blank">Google Developer Groups Warri - GDG Warri</a> which I had the honor of founding in 2018, hosted its annual community-led tech conference - <a href="https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-warri-presents-devfest-warri-2026/" target="_blank">DevFest Warri 2026</a> with the theme "**Build, Secure, Scale: Developers and Builders in the Agentic Era.**"

## A look at the Google Developer Groups Warri - GDG Warri

The <a href="https://gdg.community.dev/" target="_blank">Google Developer Groups - GDG </a>, are a community-led, local chapter of developers, students, designers, and tech enthusiats interested in Google's technologies and APIs. 

The GDG Warri is a part of the global Google Developer Groups. We began our local chapter in Delta State in 2018. You can learn more about <a href="https://medium.com/free-code-camp/how-i-started-a-google-developers-group-gdg-chapter-in-warri-nigeria-and-reached-100-members-22cbd622d070?sharedUserId=charlesfreeborn" target="_blank">the evolution of the GDG Warri here</a>.


## The Sessions at DevFest Warri 2026
![Google Developer Expert on Cloud AI, Shadrack Inusah](/images/posts/devfestwarri26/gde.jpg)
<p class="image-caption">Google Developer Expert on Cloud AI, Shadrack Inusah speaking at DevFest Warri 2026 on Build, Secure, and Scale.</p>

We had an amazing line-up of speakers for this year's DevFest including having in person, for the first time, a Google Developer Expert from Accra Ghana, Shadrack Inusah.

Here are the session that were covered at DevFest Warri 2026.

* **Ship an MCP server from your OpenAPI Spec** by Constance Etiosa, Developer Advocate at Moniepoint.
* **Technical Debt nobody talks about: the decisions that quietly slow down your project** by Covenant Popoola.
* **DevFest in 15 minutes** by Charles Freeborn.
![DevFest in 15 minutes by Charles Freeborn](/images/posts/devfestwarri26/charlesfreeborn.jpg)
<p class="image-caption"> DevFest in 15 minutes by Charles Freeborn </p>

* **Shipping AI to Production: Backend Patterns, Cloud Deployment and Live Demo** by Kingsley Akindele.
* **Build, Secure, Scale: Engineering for a world of AI agents** by Shadrack Inusah, Google Developer Expert
* Panel/Fireside **Tech as an engine for security, employment and job creation** - featuring Oghenetejiri Omojimite, Tantita Security Services Nigeria Ltd, and Charles Othuke. Moderator: Charles Freeborn.
* **AI and the future of Design** by Philip Chidera.

## Highlights and Lessons from DevFest Warri 2026
Some major highlights for us from this year's DevFest Warri was hosting the event at the <a href="https://w3wcreativehub.com/" target="_blank">W3W Creative Hub</a> - a soace that reflects our commitment to creating opportunities for technology, creativity, innovation, and collaboration in Delta State, the Niger Delta region, and Nigeria.

### Connecting Students with the Tech Ecosystem

![USSU at DevFest Warri](/images/posts/devfestwarri26/devfestussu.jpg)
<p class="image-caption">A group photo with students of Ugborikoko Secondary School at DevFest Warri</p>
Another highlight was having in attendance students of my alma mater - the Ugborikoko Secondary School, Effurun, Dleta State.

Their participation was a reminder of why community-led technology initatives matter. By introducing these students to developers, industry professionals, and Artificial Intelligence, we can help them see the possiblities available to them and encourage them to begin exploring careers in tech.

### Building Bridges across Industries

We were also pleased to welcome representatives from <a href="https://tantita.com/" target="_blank">Tantita Security Nigeria Ltd</a>, <a href="https://pindfoundation.org/" target="_blank">Foundation for Partnership Initiatives in the Niger Delta - PIND</a>, and <a href="https://www.matonengineering.com/" target="_blank">Maton Engineering Nigeria Ltd</a>.

Their presence added an important dimension to the event, creating opportunities for conversations about how we can influence the Niger Delta region.

## Join the GDG Warri

DevFest is one part of what we do at GDG Warri. Throughout the year, we continue to create opportunities for developers, students, and technology enthusiasts to learn, connect, and collaborate through workshops, meetups, hackathons, and other community activities.
